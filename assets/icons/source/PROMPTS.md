## medallion.png — основная генерация

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: production app icon foreground for ORDO — Keepers of the Ember, a cooperative Kyrgyz felt tabletop fantasy game, for Android and iOS.
Create ONE exquisite simple circular handmade felt medallion, perfectly front-facing and centered on a genuinely transparent background. The entire circular medallion fits inside 84% of square canvas width and height with transparent margin.
Subject: a single bold sculptural golden amber flame with three flowing tips and a warm ivory inner flame, on a deep burgundy felt disk, surrounded by a thick cream wool cord rim with fine ochre stitches. Near the lower left and right of the flame, two small restrained symmetrical Kyrgyz shyrdak ram-horn curlicues in muted ochre felt. The flame dominates and remains instantly recognizable at tiny app icon size.
Style: premium tactile stop-motion miniature, refined needle-felt wool fibers, clean broad shapes, gentle physical depth, warm soft lighting, rich wine red, gold and cream. Keep design simplified and high contrast, like an iconic emblem, not a busy illustration.
Constraints: only one circular medallion; absolutely no lettering, words, letters, numbers, watermark, people, scenery, mockup, square tile, extra rings outside the rim or drop shadow outside the medallion. Perfect circular silhouette, straight-on camera, transparent exterior. Square 1024x1024 output.
