# Quiet main-menu auditions

Generated with ACE Music / ACE-Step V1.5 XL Turbo on 2026-09-14.
These supersede the busier initial and v2 directions for audition.

| Files | Source direction | Requested tempo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01-in-the-end-a/b | Distant bittersweet melodic memory | 64 BPM |
| 02-sugar-a/b | Distant playful question-and-answer melody | 62 BPM |
| 03-babushka-a/b | Distant warm wistful melody | 60 BPM |

Each is a 144-second instrumental remix of the corresponding first generated
take in the parent directory, not a sampled commercial recording.
ACE Remix settings: Cover Strength 0.60; Remix Strength 0.45.

Generation direction: very quiet, soft, unobtrusive Kyrgyz acoustic background
music; short low-register phrases and breathing pauses; one or two voices at
a time; kyl kyyak, choor/chopo choor, sparse komuz; intimate dry room and natural
bow/breath texture. No percussion, drum beat, ostinato, vocals, synthesizer pads,
dramatic swells, forceful attacks or busy virtuoso melody.
Negative prompt: drums, percussion, beat, shakers, cymbals, snare, energetic,
loud, epic, cinematic, synthesizer, vocals, busy melody.

AAC files preserve the service output (M4A container despite its .aac suffix).
MP3 auditions apply only constant gain targeting -25 LUFS, a 0.8-second fade-in,
and a four-second fade-out. No dynamic compression or tempo/pitch alteration.
`prepare_previews.py` reproduces this and checks full-file decoding/loudness;
`validation.json` records measured results. These are auditions, not seamless
loop exports. Cultural/instrument fidelity and percussion absence are requested
generation properties, not established by file or loudness checks.

No tracks were published or installed as the game's active menu theme.
