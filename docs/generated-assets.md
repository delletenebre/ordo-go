# Авторские ассеты визуальной доработки

Все перечисленные текстуры созданы встроенным image_gen для ORDO. Ссылки на файлы находятся внутри проекта; внешних загрузок во время игры нет. Магазинные материалы в итоговую версию не включены. Главный художественный ориентир — предоставленная пользователем арена с шырдаком, войлочными фишками и очагом. Ниже сохранены точные промпты.

## assets/wool-detail.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Production material asset: seamless square grayscale wool FELT texture, orthographic macro, evenly lit neutral medium gray. Entire image filled edge to edge with densely tangled soft matte short sheep-wool fibers, fine curving individual strands visibly overlapping in a needle-felt surface, softly matted handmade material. Large enough fibers to remain legible when mapped onto a small game token, natural mildly clumped fibers and gentle tonal variation. No weave grid, no knitted loops, no long hair, no sparkles, no directional lighting, no shadows from objects, no vignette, no borders, no text, no objects. Seamless tiling on all four edges, uniform grey between approximately 0.35 and 0.75 luminance. This is an albedo/height detail map to tint blue, red, green and gold in a 3D game shader. High quality tactile soft wool felt.

## assets/stone-detail.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production seamless square material texture for the stone blocks of a miniature handmade fantasy game, not a scene or mockup. Fill the entire image edge to edge with a single weathered grey slate stone surface. Touchable warm-grey and blue-grey mineral material, broad irregular fractured planes, small rounded chips, hairline crevices, shallow layered stone grain, artisan miniature stop-motion realism. Moderately coarse details that remain legible on small 3D blocks. Subtle sparse woolly microfibers embedded in the matte mineral surface to belong in a felt diorama. Neutral even diffuse lighting with no highlights and no cast shadows. Midtone cool-neutral grey around 50% brightness, recesses about 30%, raised planes 65%; not black and not brown. Random organic natural stone detail, no grid, no brick pattern, no mortar, no objects, no borders, no vignette, no text. Seamlessly tile on every edge. This texture is applied to separately modeled bevelled masonry stones and hearth blocks.

## assets/shyrdak-v2.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production albedo texture, square 2048x2048 for a round 3D game-board floor. Reference is style guide only; do NOT reproduce the TV, phones, actors, UI, torches, stone walls or fire. Generate ONLY a perfectly top-down orthographic circular layered wool felt rug, whole circle centered fills 96% width, on transparent background. Follow the reference arena rug very closely: concentric multiple ivory, burgundy-red and desaturated petrol-blue rings, dense Kyrgyz shyrdak ram-horn ornamental motifs, a large geometric eight-point cream/red star medallion across the main middle playing field, inner dark teal ring around a small empty charcoal-teal center, outer broad cream-and-rust embroidered band. Rug reads predominantly pale warm cream and muted burgundy rather than a broad flat orange-red field. Extremely tactile thick coarse felt fibers and layered cut felt applique, yarn stitching around the concentric rings, slightly worn edges, hundreds of fine hand stitches, intricate yet readable pattern. Neutral diffuse lighting, no cast shadows, no perspective, no objects or flames, no text, no UI. This is a material map to place onto real geometry and light in-engine. Preserve the authentic texture and colors of the reference circular arena.

## assets/player-caps-v2.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: actual game albedo atlas for FOUR player puck top surfaces. The attached image is the authoritative reference. Reproduce the FOUR PLAYER PUCKS visible on that reference: the blue triangle, amber-gold hexagon, emerald-green diamond, crimson-red ring. Do not invent cute knitted buttons. Exactly 2x2 equal square cells, blue top-left, gold top-right, green bottom-left, red bottom-right. All pucks seen exactly TOP-DOWN orthographic, equal diameter 86% of each cell, centered. Match reference construction: low dense needle-felt game pucks, broad pale colored felt backing rim, dark recessed circular seam, smaller saturated colored central pad with gently convex bevel. FINE compact short matted fibers, restrained material variation; not shaggy, not thick knitted fabric, NO chunky rope. Small very fine dark stitches only in the recessed seam, absolutely NO large ivory decorative running stitches around the disk. Icon is a THIN smooth light-ivory outlined geometric glyph inset into the central pad with a delicate pale glow and a slightly dark colored inner edge; line thickness only about 2.5 percent of puck diameter, not a thick rope or twisted yarn. Triangle points UP. Diamond centered. Hexagon gold. Ring red. Tone is polished tactile fantasy tabletop game exactly as in reference, not a nursery toy. Separate clean transparent background; no text, no UI, no camera perspective, no walls, no shadows beyond disks, no other objects. Keep all four disks within own cells.

## Музыка и звуки

27 оригинальных WAV-файлов созданы scripts-independent генератором `tools/generate_audio.py`, без сторонних семплов. Две бесшовные музыкальные петли: menu.wav — 64 секунды, interlude.wav — 32 секунды; собственная мелодия и модель щипковой струны, вдохновлённая тембром комуза. Это синтезированная стилизация, а не запись настоящего инструмента или традиционного кюу. Остальные файлы — интерфейс, удары, скольжение, огонь, способности, реакции монстров и переходы. 24 кГц, PCM16 stereo. Генератор воспроизводим и использует только стандартную библиотеку Python.
